The performance division

ttSport builds the version the engineers keep.

Every ttSport car leaves the line with a biturbo V8, a widened track, a seven-speed dual-clutch transmission and three additional drive modes. The suffix is applied to a model code. It is never sold on its own.

Manufacturing

Aluminium where it earns its cost.

The body in white is a mixed structure: aluminium for the bonnet, doors, boot lid and front longitudinal members, high-strength steel for the passenger cell. An 856x Touring weighs 1 705 kg with a full tank. The saloon it is derived from weighs 1 630 kg.

Every shell is measured at 214 points before it leaves the body shop. The tolerance is ±0,5 mm.
