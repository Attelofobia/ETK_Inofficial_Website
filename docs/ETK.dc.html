ttSport

The division that signs its work on the boot lid.

ttSport has operated as a separate works since 1989. It takes a finished ETK from the line, removes the powertrain and the suspension, and returns the car with its own.

Manufacturing

Aluminium where it earns its cost.

The body in white is a mixed structure: aluminium for the bonnet, doors, boot lid and front longitudinal members, high-strength steel for the passenger cell. An 856 Touring weighs 1 705 kg with a full tank. The saloon it is derived from weighs 1 630 kg.

Every shell is measured at 214 points before it leaves the body shop. The tolerance is ±0,5 mm.

Proving ground · 02:40

Every model year ends here.

The night shift signs off the lighting, the tail lamp signature and the door projection on all five bodies before the year is released.
